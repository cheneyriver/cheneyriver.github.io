  <p>I am an MPhil student in the AI Thrust, Information Hub, at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by <strong>Hao Wang</strong>. My research focuses on <strong>LLM agents, multi-agent collaboration, and agent memory</strong>.</p>
  <p>I am particularly interested in multi-agent systems, especially how agents interact, communicate, and coordinate with one another in different settings. By investigating these dynamics, I hope to deepen our understanding of AI agents. This also leads to my interest in <strong>AI safety and AI governance</strong>: ensuring that increasingly powerful AI systems benefit humanity, and establishing clear and reliable boundaries for their behavior, will be critically important in the future.</p>
