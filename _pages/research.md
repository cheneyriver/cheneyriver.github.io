## Earlier research

### Scientific mobility across borders

**Research Assistant · Fudan University · February–December 2024**<br>
Advisor: Meijun Liu

I investigated cross-border mobility patterns of AI talent during the COVID-19 pandemic, processing over **10 million publication and address records** using Python, Geopy, and LLMs. This work contributed to *Scientists Moving across Borders during the Pandemic*, accepted at the Conference on Science and Innovation Policy 2025.

### Legal information seeking on video platforms

**Research Assistant · City University of Hong Kong · May–September 2024**<br>
Advisor: Zhicong Lu

I studied legal influencers and legal information-seeking behavior on Chinese short-form video platforms. The research involved semi-structured interviews with **20 users** and thematic analysis, contributing to *“Law at Your Fingertips”*, accepted at the 29th ACM Conference on Computer-Supported Cooperative Work and Social Computing.
